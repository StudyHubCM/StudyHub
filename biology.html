🟢 O/L Biology — 0510

Follow the topics in this order for complete O/L Biology preparation.

🌱 1. The Living World

Characteristics of living organisms, organization, classification, biodiversity and conservation.

🔬 2. Cells & Microscopy

Cell theory, plant and animal cells, organelles, specialized cells, microscopes, magnification and biological drawings.

🧪 3. Biological Molecules & Food

Carbohydrates, proteins, lipids, water, mineral salts, balanced diet, food tests, deficiency diseases, malnutrition and food hygiene.

🍽️ 4. Nutrition & Digestion

Modes of nutrition, digestion, absorption, assimilation, small intestine, teeth and dental health.

🌿 5. Plant Nutrition

Photosynthesis, chlorophyll, requirements for photosynthesis, experiments and mineral nutrition.

💨 6. Respiration

Aerobic and anaerobic respiration, energy release, gas exchange and factors affecting respiration.

🫀 7. Transport

Blood, blood cells, plasma, blood vessels, heart, circulation, lymph, xylem, phloem and transpiration.

💧 8. Excretion & Homeostasis

Excretion, kidneys, urine formation, water balance, temperature regulation and homeostasis.

🦴 9. Movement & Support

Skeleton, functions of the skeleton, joints, synovial joints, muscles, antagonistic muscles and movement.

🧠 10. Coordination & Response

Nervous system, neurones, reflex actions, sense organs, eye defects, hormones and plant responses.

🦠 11. Disease & Health

Pathogens, communicable diseases, HIV/AIDS, malaria, immunity, hygiene, reproductive health and STIs.

🌸 12. Reproduction

Asexual and sexual reproduction, human reproduction, pregnancy, family planning, flowers, pollination, seeds and germination.

🧬 13. Genetics & Variation

Heredity, genes, chromosomes, alleles, genetic crosses, variation, mutation and natural selection.

🌍 14. Ecology & Environment

Habitats, populations, communities, ecosystems, food chains, energy flow, nutrient cycles, pollution and conservation.

🧫 15. Microorganisms & Biotechnology

Bacteria, fungi, viruses, useful and harmful microorganisms, fermentation, genetic engineering and cloning.

🔬 16. Practical Biology

Biological drawings, food tests, experiments, data tables, graphs, variables, observations, conclusions and specimen identification.
