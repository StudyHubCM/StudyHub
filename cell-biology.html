🧬 Cell Biology

Learn the basic structure and functions of cells.

What is a Cell?

A cell is the basic structural and functional unit of life. Living organisms are made up of one or more cells.

Cell Structures

Cells contain structures called organelles. Different organelles perform different functions within the cell.

🧠 Key Points

  • Cells are the basic units of life.
  • Cells contain specialised structures called organelles.
  • Different organelles have different functions.

Quick Revision

Remember: structure is closely related to function. Understanding what an organelle looks like and what it does helps you understand how cells work.
