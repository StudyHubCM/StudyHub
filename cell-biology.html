🧬 Cell Biology

Learn the basic structure and functions of cells.

1. What is a Cell?

A cell is the basic structural and functional unit of life. This means that cells form the bodies of living organisms and carry out the processes needed for life.

Some organisms consist of only one cell, while others are made up of many different types of cells working together.

2. Cell Theory

Cell theory is a group of ideas that explains the importance of cells in living organisms.

🧠 Key Points

  • All living organisms are made up of one or more cells.
  • The cell is the basic unit of structure and function in living organisms.
  • New cells come from existing cells.

3. Types of Cells

Cells can be grouped into two major types: prokaryotic cells and eukaryotic cells.

🔬 Prokaryotic Cells

Prokaryotic cells are generally simpler cells. They do not have a nucleus surrounded by a membrane. Bacteria are examples of organisms made up of prokaryotic cells.

🧬 Eukaryotic Cells

Eukaryotic cells contain a nucleus surrounded by a membrane. They also contain several specialised organelles. Animals, plants, fungi and many other organisms have eukaryotic cells.

4. Cell Organelles

Organelles are specialised structures inside cells. Each organelle performs particular functions that help the cell survive and work properly.

⚙️ Important Organelles

  • Nucleus: Contains genetic material and controls many activities of the cell.
  • Mitochondria: Release energy through aerobic respiration.
  • Ribosomes: Make proteins.
  • Cell membrane: Controls the movement of substances into and out of the cell.
  • Cytoplasm: Where many chemical reactions take place.
  • Chloroplasts: Found in photosynthetic plant cells and contain chlorophyll.
  • Cell wall: Provides support and helps maintain the shape of plant cells.
  • Vacuole: In plant cells, the large permanent vacuole contains cell sap and helps maintain pressure inside the cell.

5. Animal and Plant Cells

Animal and plant cells are both eukaryotic cells, so they share many structures. However, plant cells have some additional structures that are important for their functions.

6. Quick Revision

🧠 Remember

  • Cells are the basic units of life.
  • Prokaryotic cells do not have a membrane-bound nucleus.
  • Eukaryotic cells have a membrane-bound nucleus.
  • Organelles have specialised functions.
  • Plant and animal cells share many structures, but plant cells also have structures such as chloroplasts and a cell wall.

Quick Revision
