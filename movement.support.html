🦴 Movement & Support

Support, movement, skeleton, joints, muscles and movement in plants.

🦴 1. Introduction to Movement and Support

Movement is the ability of an organism or part of an organism to change position. Support is the ability to maintain the shape and position of the body.

💪 2. Movement & Support in Humans

In humans, movement is produced by the coordinated action of bones, joints and muscles.
